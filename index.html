Yes - Greater Water delivers potable drinking water only. Our trucks are dedicated to potable water and are not used to carry non-drinking water. All water is collected from council drinking-water fill stations and has already been treated for potable use. Yes - we regularly deliver to farms and rural properties. Our trucks are equipped with features including differential locks to help access more difficult rural sites. If access may be challenging, please mention this when you call so we can plan the delivery appropriately.

Water delivery starts from approximately $350. Final pricing depends on factors such as your delivery location, the quantity of water required and site access. Please call Greater Water for a delivery price for your property.

We always aim to deliver same day where we can, depending on availability and location. At busier times, delivery can generally be arranged within 24 hours. You can call 24 hours a day, 7 days a week to enquire about water delivery.

Please have handy:

  • An approximate distance from where the truck can park to the tank
